# Mystery shop protocol (TOL-2916)

**Status: DRAFT. Not approved. No shop has been made.** Approval order: Susan (client), then Jeramey. Two shops: S1 a hot tub or swim spa, S2 a stove, insert or venting job. Each is one real inquiry by a person nobody at the shop knows; an agent never makes the call or the visit.

## Rules

- Ask real questions about a real need. No made-up address, no fake name that could land on a lead list: the shopper gives a first name and a callback only if asked, and says "just looking".
- Never buy, put down a deposit, sign or book work. Nothing is committed on the shop's behalf.
- Never ask the shop for a review or a rating, and never offer one.
- Never pay or take anything for the shop. If staff quote a price, write it in the notes; do not negotiate.
- Score within the hour, from notes, before talking to anyone at the shop about it.
- Within a week tell Susan and Cam that shops were made, in general terms. Scores go to Susan, not to the person who was shopped, unless Susan says otherwise.
- Log the row in the tracker: kind `shop`, `notes_location`, the total score out of 20 in `themes`, never a name.

## Scorecard

Each line is scored 0 (missed), 1 (partly), 2 (done well). Total out of 20. A score is a read of one interaction, not a verdict on a person.

- M1. The call or visit was answered or greeted within a minute.
- M2. They asked what I wanted to do before telling me what to buy.
- M3. They asked about my home or site (deck, chimney, clearances, power).
- M4. They explained the options in plain words, with no pressure.
- M5. They said what a safe install or venting needs, without being asked.
- M6. They were straight about what they do not sell or do.
- M7. They gave a next step I could take (a visit, a site check, a call back with a time).
- M8. They did not quote a price they could not stand behind, or said who confirms it.
- M9. They offered to follow up and asked how, not just for a number.
- M10. I would call them back.

## Notes to capture

Date and time, who answered (role, not name), what they asked me, what they told me, how long it took, and anything that surprised me. Full notes go to the client's OneDrive. Nothing about a person leaves the notes except their role.
