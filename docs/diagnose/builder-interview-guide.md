# Builder interview guide (TOL-2916)

**Status: DRAFT. Not approved. Not used on a real call.** Approval order: Susan (client), then Jeramey. Each builder is introduced by Cam first.

Twenty-five minutes, hard stop. Five of these. Builders are the second most credible growth line (see `../outreach/builder-program-kit.md`); this finds out what is true before the trade terms are offered to anyone.

## Rules

- Cam's introduction comes first. Nobody is cold-called.
- Open with who you are, that this is research, that it takes about twenty-five minutes, and that they can stop.
- Do not present trade terms, prices or a percentage. The builder kit is approved separately and nothing in it is shown on this call.
- Never quote a price, lead time or warranty, and never ask for a review, rating or referral.
- Never offer a gift, credit or discount for taking part.
- Log the row in the tracker before the next call: kind `builder`.

## Open

> Hi, is this {{first_name}}? It's {{interviewer}}. Cam suggested I call. We're asking the builders and remodelers he works with how fire, venting and hot tub jobs go for them, so we can do it better. It takes about twenty-five minutes and you can stop any time. Is now OK, and may I take notes?

## Questions

- B1. Tell me about the last job where you needed a stove, fireplace, insert or hot tub supplied and installed. Who did what?
- B2. Where do you buy that kind of equipment, and why there?
- B3. What goes wrong most often between the order and a passed inspection?
- B4. How do you find out about stock and lead times, and how reliable is it?
- B5. What does a supplier do that makes you go back, and what makes you stop?
- B6. How do you want the install handled: your crew, theirs, or a split?
- B7. How do you pay suppliers, and what matters to you about how that works?
- B8. What would make you send us more of your jobs?
- B9. Is there anything I should have asked and did not?

## Close

> Thank you, that was useful. Cam will hear what we learn across everyone we talked to. Take care.

## If they say stop

> Understood, thank you. We won't call again about this.

Set `status` to `declined` in the tracker and tell Cam.
